We are pleased to welcome Tianyuan as a Summer Research Intern. Tianyuan is a second-year undergraduate student in Biomedical Engineering at the National University of Singapore, one of Asia’s leading research universities.

During her time in the lab, Tianyuan will participate in research projects focused on precision nanomedicine, RNA therapeutics, and lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-mediated drug and gene delivery. Through this experience, she will gain hands-on training in cutting-edge biomedical research and contribute to ongoing efforts to develop next-generation therapeutics for inflammatory and pulmonary diseases.

We are excited to have Tianyuan join our team and look forward to supporting her scientific growth and research development. We warmly welcome her to the Zhou Lab and wish her a productive, rewarding, and inspiring summer research experience.
