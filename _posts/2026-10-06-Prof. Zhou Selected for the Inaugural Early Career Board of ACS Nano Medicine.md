---
title: Prof. Zhou Selected for the Inaugural Early Career Board of ACS Nano Medicine
author: Zhou Lab
layout: post
group: news
---

We are pleased to announce that Prof. Zhou has been selected to serve on the inaugural Early Career Board of ACS Nano Medicine. The board was chosen through a highly competitive process that attracted more than 300 applications from early-career researchers worldwide.

During the two-year term, Prof. Zhou will serve as an ambassador for the journal, contribute perspectives on emerging research areas, and support engagement with authors and readers across the nanomedicine community.

Zhou lab looks forward to contributing to the journal’s growth and helping shape its engagement with the next generation of nanomedicine researchers.
