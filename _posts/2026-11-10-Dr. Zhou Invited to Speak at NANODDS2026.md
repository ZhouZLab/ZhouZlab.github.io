Dr. Zhou has been invited to speak at the 24th International Nanomedicine and Drug Delivery Symposium (nanoDDS 2026), to be held in Oklahoma City.

The Nanomedicine and Drug Delivery Symposium is a long-standing international conference in the field of nanomedicine and advanced drug delivery, bringing together leading researchers and innovators to discuss emerging technologies and translational advances in nanotherapeutics.

Recognized for his expertise in nanomedicine and mRNA/lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery, Dr. Zhou has been invited to present in the Lipid Nanoparticle Session. Dr. Zhou looks forward to sharing the lab’s latest research and engaging with leaders, colleagues, and trainees in the nanomedicine and drug delivery community.
