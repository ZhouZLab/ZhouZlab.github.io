Dr. Zhengjie Zhou was invited to speak at the Chinese Association for Science and Technology, USA (CAST-USA) 34th Annual Conference & 2026 Global Innovation Summit, held on October 31, 2026, in Philadelphia.

As an invited speaker in the Medicine & Healthcare Forum, Dr. Zhou presented the lab’s research and shared perspectives on emerging advances in biomedical research and nanomedicine. The forum provided an excellent opportunity to exchange ideas with physician leaders, biomedical scientists, and AI innovators and to explore new opportunities for interdisciplinary research and collaboration.
